## Full description (4000 max)
Couplemetric turns your relationship into something you can actually see and improve.

Every day, each partner answers a 2-minute check-in. Couplemetric scores the relationship across 9 dimensions — emotional connection, trust, fairness, stress, autonomy, vitality, growth, security and connection — and shows you the trend over time.

WHAT YOU GET FOR FREE

• Streaks to keep you both consistent
• 7-day history

• Partner linking: compare how you each see the relationship

PREMIUM (one subscription covers both partners)
• 90-day history and trends
• Optimizer insights: which dimension to work on next for the biggest gain
• Full exercise library
• Partner divergence breakdown: where you see things differently
• Scenario modes for exams, travel, crisis, new baby and more
• AI coaching (30 messages per day)
• Export and therapist-ready reports

WHY IT WORKS
Most couples apps give you prompts. Couplemetric gives you data. A real scoring engine and an optimizer (yes, actual math) highlight the one thing that will move your score most this week — so effort goes where it matters.
